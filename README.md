#  Superstore Data Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard** built using Power BI. The goal is to extract meaningful business insights from retail data and support data-driven decision-making.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales performance across regions, categories, customers, and time.

---

##  Business Objectives

* Analyze overall sales, profit, and quantity performance
* Identify top-performing regions and product categories
* Evaluate customer contribution to revenue
* Track monthly sales trends and seasonality
* Highlight low-performing sub-categories

---


## Key Insights

* The **West region** contributes approximately **35% of total sales**, making it the leading region
* The **Technology category** generates the highest revenue among all categories
* **Copiers** are the most profitable sub-category, indicating strong margins
* Sales show a **steady upward trend**, with a peak during **Q4**
* Certain sub-categories like **Tables and Bookcases** contribute lower profitability

---

## Business Impact

* Enables stakeholders to identify high-revenue and high-profit segments
* Supports strategic decision-making for regional expansion
* Helps optimize product portfolio by focusing on profitable categories
* Assists in identifying underperforming areas for improvement
